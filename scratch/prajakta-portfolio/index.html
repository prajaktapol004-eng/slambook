Wireless & Security

Smart Encrypted Walkie-Talkie

A decentralized, direct RF point-to-point voice communication device built for environments lacking cellular or internet infrastructure. Employs low-latency wireless protocol and cryptographic ciphering to safeguard real-time audio transmission.

  • ✦ RF Protocol: Built on ESP-NOW peer-to-peer wireless packet transmission for ultra-low latency.
  • ✦ Cryptographic Security: AES encryption applied to digitized voice payloads before RF broadcast.
  • ✦ Audio Interfacing: I2S digital microphone module and voice-activation (VOX) logic for hands-free control.
